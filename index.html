  • Healthy-Reference-Guided Brain MRI Classification

    Thesis · in progress

    A four-class brain MRI classifier: Healthy, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's and Multiple Sclerosis.

    • Adapts ImageNet-pretrained ConvNeXt-Tiny and Swin-Tiny to MRI through masked-autoencoder self-supervised domain adaptation.
    • A Healthy Reference Memory of 8 learnable prototypes, retrieved by cross-attention, feeding a Kolmogorov–Arnold Network head for interpretable predictions.
    • Training on public OASIS and Kaggle MRI datasets; benchmark results pending.
